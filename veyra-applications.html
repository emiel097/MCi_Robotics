APPLICATIONS

One drive system. Four places it goes to work.

From assistive robotics in the home to light industrial automation and mobile logistics, the same compact, low-cost actuator adapts to the task in front of it — the arm changes, Veyra doesn't have to.

APPLICATION AREA 1

Independent Living & Home Care

A robotic kitchen assistant built on Veyra actuators gives people an extra pair of hands for everyday cooking — retrieving ingredients, handling containers, and taking on repetitive tasks. It's built to support independence, not replace it: the person stays in control of the process from start to finish.

Living independently

As an elderly person who wants to stay active and self-sufficient, I use a robotic kitchen assistant to help with everyday cooking — retrieving ingredients, handling containers, and taking on repetitive tasks. It gives me an extra pair of hands so I can prepare home-cooked meals safely and confidently, on my own.

Reduced mobility

As a person with reduced mobility, I use voice commands to control a robotic arm that helps with everyday kitchen tasks. It extends my reach, helping me retrieve ingredients, handle tools, and get through repetitive tasks — so I can keep preparing home-cooked meals safely and independently, with less reliance on others.

APPLICATION AREA 2

Home Cooking Assistance

Not everyone needs assistance out of necessity — some just want more time for the parts of cooking they enjoy. A Veyra-powered arm takes on the repetitive, time-consuming steps, so the person stays focused on the creative side of the process.

Cooking for pleasure

As someone who enjoys preparing meals at home, I use voice commands to control a robotic arm that helps with repetitive, time-consuming kitchen tasks — retrieving ingredients, holding containers, moving things around the kitchen. It's an extra hand that lets me focus on the creative side of cooking, while I stay in full control of the preparation.

APPLICATION AREA 3

Light-Duty Industrial Automation

Veyra brings affordable automation to simple handling tasks in low-volume production — the gap where traditional industrial robots are too expensive or too complex to justify.

  • Pick-and-place operations
  • Sorting operations
  • Inspection support
  • End-of-line handling
  • Operator assistance
Manufacturing engineer

As a manufacturing engineer, I use a robotic arm to automatically remove rejected products from an end-of-line inspection process and place them into a rejection bin — reducing manual handling and improving process consistency.

APPLICATION AREA 4

Mobile Assistance & Logistics

Mounted on a mobile platform or AGV, a Veyra-powered arm combines mobility and manipulation: the platform handles transport, the arm handles physical interaction with the environment. Together, they take on tasks that are difficult, inefficient, or unsafe for people to do themselves.

  • Picking and placing objects
  • Material transport
  • Loading and unloading
  • Inventory handling
  • Inspection support
  • Delivery of tools or materials
  • Remote operation in hazardous environments
Warehouse operations

As a warehouse operator, I use a mobile robotic arm to pick items from shelves and load them onto an AGV for transport across the floor — cutting down on manual handling in areas that are repetitive, hard to reach, or better left to a machine.
