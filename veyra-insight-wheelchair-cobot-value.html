Concise Customer Story

“I can help my grandchildren bake cookies.”

Peter uses a powered wheelchair and values his independence. He can manage many parts of daily life, but frequently has to wait for help when an object is out of reach, difficult to hold or unsafe to handle alone. These small dependencies gradually take away control over when and how ordinary activities happen.

A wheelchair-mounted cobot gives Peter a trusted third hand. It retrieves ingredients, holds a bowl, moves lightweight items and supports tasks that would otherwise require another person. The technology does not take over the activity. Peter remains in control.

Before

Present, but unable to participate fully

When his grandchildren bake cookies, Peter may have to watch, wait or ask someone else to perform each physical step.

With the Cobot

Actively contributing to the moment

Peter can retrieve ingredients, carry utensils and hold items safely, allowing him to join the activity and help his grandchildren.

Functional Outcome

Everyday tasks become more accessible

The cobot supports reaching, holding, carrying, retrieving and manipulating objects safely throughout the day.

Human Outcome

Peter can be a grandfather, not only a care recipient

The most valuable result is restored agency, belonging and the ability to contribute to the people around him.

“The biggest difference is not that the robot helps me. It is that I no longer have to wait for help. I can do things when I want and be part of the moments that matter.”

Stakeholder Value

One system, different forms of value

The user receives the direct benefit, while family members, caregivers, service providers and funders each experience a different outcome from the same capability.

01

Wheelchair User

More control over daily routines, safer access to objects and the ability to participate and contribute without waiting for support.

Core value: agency, dignity and belonging.
02

Family and Informal Caregivers

Less routine assistance and greater confidence that their loved one has practical support when nobody else is present.

Core value: peace of mind and more meaningful time together.
03

Professional Caregivers

Less time spent fetching objects and supporting routine chores, leaving more time for assessment, coaching and personal contact.

Core value: more care, fewer chores.
04

Healthcare System and Insurer

Greater self-sufficiency may reduce reliance on routine support and strengthen the case for living independently for longer.

Core value: better outcomes and potential cost avoidance.
05

Maintenance and Service Provider

Diagnostics, modular replacement and accessible service points can support faster fault finding and less disruptive repairs.

Core value: predictable service and reduced downtime.
06

Bystanders and the Public

Predictable movement, clear status communication and safe physical behaviour help the cobot feel understandable and approachable.

Core value: trust, safety and social acceptance.

Actuator Platform Design Intent

Key parameters guiding the intended design

These parameters summarise the current platform direction from the MCi scope and concept documents. They are design intentions and working targets, not yet released product specifications or demonstrated production performance.

Scope boundary: MCi intends to provide actuator-level functionality. Robot kinematics, trajectory planning, robot operating software, machine vision, AI models, voice interfaces and task-level autonomy remain outside the actuator scope, while standard interfaces should enable integration with those systems.

Actuator product-platform strategy
Design Principle

Application-fit performance, not maximum industrial specification

The intended actuator platform balances cost, mass, safety, serviceability and manufacturability for human-proximate applications. The commercial test is not the actuator price alone, but whether the architecture lowers the total deployed-system cost without transferring complexity or risk elsewhere.

Core Value Proposition

Enabling people to act, participate and contribute

The wheelchair-mounted cobot is a safe and dependable helping hand that extends the user's physical capability throughout the day. It enables people with reduced mobility to carry out everyday activities when they choose, participate more fully in family life and contribute to the people around them.

MCi's role: provide the safe, quiet, efficient, reliable and scalable robotic drive technology that turns physical assistance into greater agency, dignity and participation.
