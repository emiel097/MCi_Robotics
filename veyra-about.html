The origin

We invented electric mirror adjustment.

MCi is the inventor of electric mirror glass adjustment. The same expertise in compact, quiet, durable motion went on to power folding mirrors, camera wings, active aerodynamics and charge port doors. From mirrors to motion is how we describe the journey, and robotics is the next step.

Backed by Flex · Pointed at robotics

What we learned moving cars, we now apply to robots.

MCi is part of Flex, a manufacturer with a workforce across about 30 countries. Veyra brings the discipline behind 90M+ actuators a year to a new market: drive systems for robotics and mobile systems.

Scalability
We know how to go from prototype to volume production without compromising on quality. The processes behind 90M+ actuators a year are the ones we apply to Veyra drive systems.
Cost efficiency
Manufacturing refined over 60+ years of automotive production, applied to robotics components. The result is a fraction of typical market prices.
Reliability
Automotive quality is among the toughest in the world. MCi holds its automotive production to under 3 PPM, with 100% end-of-line testing. That discipline is the standard Veyra is built to.
