PRODUCT

One drive system, plug-and-play for nearly any application.

Veyra is a compact, lightweight drive system — motor plus reduction — designed for robotics and mobile systems. With CAN bus communication and a bayonet mount, it connects fast, and swaps out just as fast.

WHY THE DIFFERENCE

Automotive scale makes it possible.

Comparable integrated servo actuators typically run €700–900 apiece. Veyra delivers similar or better torque-to-weight performance for around a sixth of that price — made possible by automotive-scale manufacturing, not by cutting corners on quality.

Applications in focus.

Same drive system, different strengths per application.

Cobots & humanoids
Low backlash (< 0.15°) for precise, repeatable motion in joints and manipulators.
AGVs
Low current draw extends operating time per battery charge in mobile platforms.
Wheelchairs & hospital beds
IP56 protection and low heat generation for safe, long-term use close to people.
Office & workbench
Compact bayonet mount enables fast installation and maintenance, no special tools required.
